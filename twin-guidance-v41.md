# Twin spatial guidance v41

The canonical aquarium twin is the shared visual surface for maintenance, placement and demonstrations.

Required API:
- `highlightZone({id,label,position,size})`
- `clearHighlights()`
- `previewEquipment({id,type,position,sizeCm,label})`

Initial operational use: highlight a bounded substrate zone before a partial siphon. Later the same contract is used for fan/pump/light placement, cable routing, coral placement and compatibility demonstrations.
